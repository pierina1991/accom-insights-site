Does CONFOTUR apply to a villa you build for yourself in Punta Cana?

By ACCOM · Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · November 2026

Generally no: CONFOTUR benefits attach to projects certified under Law 158-01, usually a developer's, not to an individual's villa on an independently bought lot. But a buyer inside Puntacana Resort & Club may hold something different, and most pages answering this question do not say what. We are ACCOM - construction and project management in Punta Cana, with 11 projects delivered since 2006 and Park View, a fully funded 60-apartment development, in construction at Puntacana West Village. This is general information, not tax or legal advice.

What is CONFOTUR and who is it actually for?

CONFOTUR is the incentive regime created by Law 158-01 to promote tourism development. In the way it is generally described, it works through a certified project: the developer obtains certification, and benefits flow to that project and its buyers. The benefits usually quoted are relief from the 3% property transfer tax and from the annual property tax (IPI) for up to 15 years, with the remaining years passing to a later buyer. The point that matters here is the first one: the certificate belongs to the project.

Does CONFOTUR apply to a villa I build for myself on my own lot?

Generally no. If you buy a lot independently and build for yourself, there is no certified project to attach the benefits to. Almost every law firm and realtor page says the same thing, and we agree with it. What they leave out is the second question: what do you hold if the lot is inside a resort community?

What tax benefits does an original buyer at Puntacana Resort & Club actually have?

The resort's own published buyer FAQ, dated July 2017, says the original buyer inside Puntacana Resort & Club is exempt from the 3% transfer tax under Laws 158-01 and 195-13. We cite it as what the resort has published, not as current legal advice: confirm it against your own purchase agreement and with your lawyer, because your position depends on whether you are the original buyer and on how the lot is titled.

Can a lot inside a resort carry CONFOTUR through the resort's own certified project?

Only if that project is certified and the certificate is current. Do not rely on a brochure line. Ask the seller for the CONFOTUR resolution number, ask your lawyer to confirm the certificate is valid and covers your lot and your transaction, and keep a copy in your closing file.

What taxes and costs remain either way?

Unless relieved, annual property tax (IPI) is 1% of appraised value above the individual exemption, which DGII lists at RD$10,695,494 for 2026 (1% of company assets if the lot is titled to a company). On top of that come the closing fee, insurance and utilities. We tabulate them in When do you have to start building on your lot at Puntacana Resort & Club?, which also covers the build-start deadline that sits next to the tax question.

What should I check before closing on a lot I plan to build on?

  • Which tax regime applies to you, in writing, and from which clause or certificate.
  • The CONFOTUR resolution number and the certificate's validity date, if one is claimed.
  • The build-start deadline in the purchase agreement, and the extension route.
  • The design rules and covenants that will shape your architect's brief.
  • The all-in budget: holding costs plus building cost per square meter.
  • A lawyer and a tax adviser, both before closing.
