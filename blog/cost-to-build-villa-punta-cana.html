What it really costs to build a villa in Punta Cana (and why 16 months is our number)

By ACCOM · Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · July 2026

For a custom villa at the quality level Punta Cana's premium communities expect, a realistic 2026 budget runs from roughly US$1,100 to US$1,400 or more per square meter, and a full custom build takes 16 months when execution is disciplined. Those are the two numbers every lot owner asks us for, so here they are in the first paragraph. The rest of this article explains what moves them.

Every week someone who just bought a lot in Puntacana Resort & Club, Cap Cana, or Corales asks us the same two questions: what will it really cost, and how long will it really take. After building here since 2006, with 11 projects delivered across the Dominican Republic, here are honest answers.

How much does it cost to build a villa in Punta Cana?

Budget realistically per square meter and treat any quote far below market as a schedule and quality risk, not a bargain. The real number is driven by three things: structure and finishes, site conditions, and how well your builder buys. Across 11 delivered projects since 2006 we have priced villas through every market cycle, and one constant holds: a builder with two decades of supplier relationships pays less for the same materials than one improvising purchases. That difference funds your pool.

How long does it take to build?

We commit to 16 months for a full custom villa and we plan every build against that schedule, milestone by milestone. Delays in the Caribbean rarely come from weather. They come from permits filed late, suppliers without priority, and decisions that reach the site slowly. Local execution is the whole game - it is why the same house can take 16 months with one team and 30 with another.

What should you ask any builder before signing?

Show me a completed project I can visit - ours include Tortuga Bay H2 (pictured above) and ten more across the DR. Show me a budget with real local prices, not allowances that balloon later. Tell me who answers when something slips. If the answers are vague, keep looking. A serious builder will also welcome your owner's representative or project manager at the table; we play that role ourselves on Park View, a fully funded 60-apartment development now in construction at Puntacana West Village.

Quick answers

Is it cheaper to build or buy in Punta Cana?

Comparable finished villas in premium communities trade well above their construction cost, so building typically creates equity - if the budget holds. That depends almost entirely on who builds it.

Can foreigners build in the Dominican Republic?

Yes. Foreigners can own titled property and build with no restrictions. The legal basics and the CONFOTUR tax regime are covered in the guide.
