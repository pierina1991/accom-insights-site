Construction cost per square meter in the Dominican Republic: 2026 numbers by tier and location

By ACCOM · Punta Cana, Dominican Republic · November 2026

Published 2026 guides put construction in the Dominican Republic between about US$650 per square meter for standard national residential building and US$3,000 or more for luxury beachfront, and Punta Cana sits at the top of that range. For a custom villa at the quality level Punta Cana's premium communities expect, ACCOM plans roughly US$1,100 to US$1,400 or more per square meter, before the second wage tranche due on 1 June 2027. We are ACCOM - construction and project management in Punta Cana, with 11 projects delivered since 2006 and Park View, a fully funded 60-apartment development, in construction at Puntacana West Village.

What does it cost per square meter to build in the Dominican Republic in 2026?

There is no official per-square-meter price. The ONE publishes an index of cost movement, not a price list, so the figures below come from market guides and are labelled with who published them. Read them as ranges, not quotes.

Why is building in Punta Cana more expensive than in Santiago or Santo Domingo?

One published comparison prices a 150 square meter house at about US$87,000 in Santiago and about US$135,000 in Punta Cana, roughly US$580 versus US$900 per square meter at that quality level. From our experience building here since 2006, the gap comes from four things: logistics for materials that arrive by road or sea, competition for skilled labor in a resort market, design rules inside premium communities, and structure built for hurricane loads.

Does the community change the price per square meter?

Less than most owners expect. In our experience quality tier, structure, pool and landscaping scope and site conditions move the number more than whether the lot is in Puntacana Resort & Club, Cap Cana, Corales or Hacienda. What a community does add is design review and covenants, which can raise specification and soften schedule, so read them before you budget. ACCOM does not publish a per-square-meter figure by community, because a number that ignores your lot and your specification would mislead you.

What moved construction prices in 2026?

The ONE's housing construction cost index (ICDV) stood at 250.81 in August 2026, up 6.42% in twelve months, with labor up 12% in August alone after a wage resolution (CNS-04-2026) that adds a further 8% on 1 June 2027. Most published ranges were set before those wage increases arrived in full. Our estimate of the effect on a custom villa budget is in construction labor costs in the Dominican Republic jumped 12% in August.

Why do published cost ranges disagree?

Three reasons. They mix quality tiers, so a standard-finish figure sits next to a luxury one. They define scope differently: some price the building only, others include pool, landscaping, site work and furniture. And they were set at different dates, many before June 2026. When two ranges look contradictory, ask which tier, which scope and which date before deciding either is wrong.

How should you use a per-square-meter number?

  • Use it to screen, not to sign. A per-square-meter figure tells you if a quote is plausible, not what your house costs.
  • Ask every bidder for a line-item budget with local prices, and for what is excluded.
  • Separate the building from the pool, landscaping, site work, permits and furnishings.
  • Ask in writing who pays for the June 2027 wage tranche.
  • Treat a quote far below the market range as a schedule and quality risk, not a bargain.
